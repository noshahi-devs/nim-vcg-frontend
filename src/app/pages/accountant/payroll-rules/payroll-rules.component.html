Automatic Salary Deduction Rule

When active, this rule is used to suggest a salary deduction on the Pay Salary form based on a staff member's absences and approved leaves for the pay period. Sundays and dates in the Holidays calendar are never counted as absences. The suggestion is always editable — nothing is deducted automatically without review.

Rule Active
Turn off to stop suggesting deductions entirely.
Per-day rate is always each staff member's own Basic Salary ÷ this number — there's no separate flat amount to configure.
Is Absent = Leave
ON: unapproved absences share the same "Leaves Allowed" pool as approved leaves — only days beyond the pool deduct.
OFF: absences always deduct in full; the pool only protects approved leave.
